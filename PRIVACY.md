# PoseGuard Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026

PoseGuard helps you keep a good posture using the motion sensors in your AirPods. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The same text is available inside the app, from the onboarding screen.

<!-- Keep in sync with PoseGuard (3.0)/View/PrivacyPolicyView.swift. -->

## What PoseGuard collects

Nothing leaves your iPhone. PoseGuard has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Motion data

While a tracking session is running, PoseGuard reads your head orientation from your AirPods through Apple's Core Motion headphone motion API. The app processes these readings in real time on your iPhone to tell whether your posture is correct. Raw motion readings are not stored.

## Session history

When you stop tracking, PoseGuard saves a summary of the session on your iPhone: its start and end time and the percentage of time your posture was correct. This history powers the report and stays in the app's local storage. It may be included in your device backups, according to your iCloud or computer backup settings. Deleting the app deletes the history.

## Notifications

Posture alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

## Permissions

PoseGuard asks for access to Motion & Fitness data and, optionally, for permission to send notifications. You can change either choice at any time in the Settings app.

## Children

PoseGuard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
